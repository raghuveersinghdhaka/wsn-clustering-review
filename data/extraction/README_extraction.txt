Structured data-extraction sheet of the review
"Comprehensive Review of Clustering Protocols in Wireless Sensor Networks and Performance Trends".

data_extraction.csv : one row per primary protocol study of the family tables (28 rows). The columns follow
  the extraction fields of the manuscript (Table 6): reference key and reference number (ref_no) in the
  manuscript, taxonomy placement D1-D6 and the assignment rule, decision variables, objective, context
  attributes, evaluation conditions, the reported results with their baselines (latency and other metrics in
  the column other_results), the quality-appraisal items Q1-Q5, the ordinal scores S_E and S_T (rubric of
  Sect. 7.1), dagger_cost_excluded (True for a study with an energy result whose decision mechanism is stated to
  run at the BS or offline, so that its cost is outside the reported energy) and comparability notes.
security_schemes.csv : one row per security-enhanced scheme of the security table (Table 21; 5 rows).

NR = not reported in the source, or not retrievable where only the abstract or the title and index record of a
study was available.
Values in quotation marks are the words of the source. "of the source" marks a figure, table or section of the
cited study.
